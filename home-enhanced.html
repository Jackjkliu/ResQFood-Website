Our Story

ResQFood began with a simple observation: perfectly good food was being wasted while people in our community went hungry. Founded by high school student Veer Agarwal, our mission started in local restaurants and has grown into a movement that has rescued over 20,000 pounds of food.

Every day, we connect businesses with excess food to local food pantries and community organizations, creating a bridge between waste and need. Our volunteers are the heart of this operation, dedicating their time to make sure no good food goes to waste.
