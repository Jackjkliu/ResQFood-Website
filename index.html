How it works

Surplus food to a nearby pantry in three steps

Restaurants and households can donate either way: request a pickup from our volunteer team, or deliver it yourself with the TableToTable app.

  1. Find your nearest pantry

    Locate the food pantry closest to you. We work with pantries across the area to make donating easy and accessible.

  2. Choose how to donate

    Request a pickup and the ResQFood team will contact you to schedule a time, or drop off food yourself through the TableToTable app.

  3. Feed your neighbors

    Your surplus reaches people facing food insecurity instead of a landfill. Either way, you're a hero.

Why volunteer with us?
